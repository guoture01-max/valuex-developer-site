Terms of Service

These terms cover the ValueX developer website and the planned HSBC sandbox test.

Development use

ValueX is a private financial analysis tool. This public website provides product and developer information. Portfolio calculations are for personal analysis and are not investment advice.

Sandbox scope

Use only HSBC-provided test profiles for the sandbox test and follow HSBC's developer terms. The planned integration reads test balances and transactions only. ValueX claims no production banking access or regulated AISP status. The AISP role in the developer portal refers solely to the sandbox test configuration.

Do not submit real account information, personal financial records, authorization responses, or credentials to this website or public channels. Use third-party services only with their permission and within their applicable terms.

The website and sandbox material are provided as available for development and evaluation. Documentation, sandbox behavior, and access may change during testing.
